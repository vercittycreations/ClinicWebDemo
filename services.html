Ear · Nose · Throat

ENT Treatment

Our ENT department provides comprehensive diagnosis and treatment for the full spectrum of ear, nose, and throat conditions. Using state-of-the-art endoscopic and audiometric equipment, our specialist ensures precise diagnosis and effective, minimally invasive treatment for patients of all ages.

Whether it's a persistent ear infection, a deviated septum affecting your breathing, or chronic tonsillitis — we have the expertise and technology to address it effectively.

  • Diagnosis and treatment of chronic ear infections and tympanic perforations
  • Endoscopic sinus surgery (FESS) for chronic sinusitis
  • Septoplasty and rhinoplasty for nasal correction
  • Tonsillectomy and adenoidectomy for recurrent infections
  • Tympanoplasty and mastoidectomy for eardrum repair
  • Paediatric ENT care for children's specific conditions
  • Voice and swallowing disorder management

02
Oncology · Head & Neck

Cancer Consultation

Head, neck, and ENT-related cancers — including oral cavity, laryngeal, pharyngeal, thyroid, and salivary gland cancers — require specialised early detection and a surgeon with both oncological knowledge and ENT surgical skill. Our specialist combines both.

Early detection dramatically improves survival outcomes. Our clinic is equipped to screen, diagnose, and develop comprehensive management plans for patients with suspected or confirmed ENT-related cancers.

  • Comprehensive head and neck cancer screening and risk assessment
  • Laryngeal cancer detection via videoendoscopy
  • Thyroid cancer evaluation with guided biopsy referral
  • Oral cavity, tongue and pharyngeal cancer diagnosis
  • Salivary gland tumour assessment and management
  • Coordinated referrals for radiation and chemotherapy teams
  • Post-treatment rehabilitation and follow-up care

03
Hearing Restoration

Cochlear Implant

Cochlear implants are life-changing devices for individuals with profound or severe sensorineural hearing loss who do not benefit sufficiently from hearing aids. Our specialist has performed over 500 successful cochlear implant surgeries — demonstrating extensive expertise in this field.

Our programme covers the complete journey: pre-implant evaluation, surgical procedure, device programming, and post-operative speech rehabilitation — giving patients and families comprehensive, structured support every step of the way.

  • Comprehensive pre-implant audiological and medical evaluation
  • Advanced cochlear implant surgery with minimally invasive techniques
  • Multi-brand device options — Cochlear, MED-EL, Advanced Bionics
  • Paediatric cochlear implants (from 12 months of age)
  • Post-operative speech therapy and hearing rehabilitation
  • Long-term device programming and follow-up support
  • Financial counselling and government scheme assistance

04
Audiometry & Rehabilitation

Hearing Loss Treatment

Hearing loss affects people of all ages and can have a profound impact on quality of life, communication, and emotional well-being. Our hearing care programme combines cutting-edge audiometric diagnostics with personalised rehabilitation strategies to help every patient hear better.

From mild conductive hearing loss to profound sensorineural deafness — our clinic provides evidence-based evaluation and treatment tailored to each individual's audiological profile and lifestyle needs.

  • Pure tone audiometry and speech audiometry evaluation
  • OAE, BERA, and ASSR testing for infants and children
  • Hearing aid trial, fitting, and counselling
  • Tinnitus evaluation and management
  • Surgical treatment for conductive hearing loss (ossiculoplasty)
  • Newborn hearing screening referrals and early intervention
Book Hearing Evaluation
05
Sinus · Allergy · Breathing

Sinus & Allergy Care

Chronic sinusitis and allergic rhinitis are among the most prevalent conditions affecting patients' daily quality of life — causing persistent headaches, nasal congestion, sneezing, and breathing difficulty. Our clinic offers a complete spectrum of evidence-based medical and surgical care for these conditions.

Our approach combines allergy testing, targeted medical management, and where necessary, minimally invasive endoscopic surgery to deliver lasting relief and restored breathing.

  • Comprehensive nasal endoscopy and CT sinus evaluation
  • Allergy skin testing and IgE blood panel analysis
  • Functional Endoscopic Sinus Surgery (FESS) for chronic cases
  • Nasal polyp removal and prevention management
  • Allergen immunotherapy and desensitisation programmes
  • Turbinate reduction for nasal airway improvement
  • Paediatric allergy and sinus management
